## Observed evidence

Give the authoritative source, exact notice/locator, actual access date, event date and date precision. A report or broken link is not proof of a withdrawal, retraction or wrongdoing.

## Prior assertion and proposed change

Paraphrase the affected assertion and the bounded correction. Do not repeat private or restricted material. Preserve uncertainty and competing evidence.

## Impact

List records, graph edges, summaries, snapshots, indexes, caches and exports potentially affected. Separate confirmed impact from investigation scope.

## Rights and retention

Identify what can be retained, what must be withheld and the actual decision basis. Preserve permissible history and redirects rather than silently rewriting provenance.

## Review, application and readback

Record actual reviewers/commits, accepted scope, applied release and readback of downstream changes. A corrected source file does not prove a consumer cache changed.

## Remaining work

Link follow-up issues and actual accepted ownership. Sensitive incidents follow SECURITY.md rather than this public template.
