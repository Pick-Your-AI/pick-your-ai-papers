## Sibling repository contracts

The mission identifies `pick-your-ai-datasets`, `pick-your-ai-applications` and `pick-your-ai-catalog-pipeline` as adjacent destinations. A planned name does not establish a deployed repository or API. Before integration, verify live existence, responsible owners, contract version and exact accepted handoff.

Dataset/benchmark directory entities remain distinct from experiments using them. Models remain distinct from papers describing them. Source adapters and release infrastructure remain distinct from source permission decisions. ## Relationship types

Native parent/sub-issue relationships express decomposition. Native blocked-by relationships are for genuine prerequisites whose missing outcome prevents execution. Informational inputs are ordinary source links. Technical compatibility and research-topic relationships belong in their respective graph, not the delivery-blocker graph.

Keep delivery prerequisites acyclic. A paper and benchmark may inform one another without blocking each other's metadata research. A missing public-use grant can block publication while discovery and source-policy research continue. Record readiness and reason separately from delivery Status.

## Handoff packet

Use [the handoff template](../templates/handoff.md). Identify producer and consumer issue, exact artifact path/hash/version, proposed fields and meanings, source/use scope, eligible projection, assumptions, validation evidence, required presentation limits, correction/revocation behavior and unresolved decisions. Record the actual receiving reviewer, reviewed commit and bounded decision.

A producer's statement that a consumer should accept an artifact is not a receiving receipt. An upstream accepted source policy is not acceptance of this new schema or a deployment. Continue independent work and keep missing receipts visible rather than manufacturing dependencies or completion.
